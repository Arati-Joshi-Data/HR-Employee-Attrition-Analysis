# HR Employee Attrition Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition using Python to identify workforce trends, understand the factors contributing to employee turnover, and provide data-driven recommendations to improve employee retention.

The analysis explores employee demographics, job roles, compensation, work experience, business travel, overtime, and other workplace factors influencing attrition.

## Business Objectives
- Understand the overall employee attrition rate.
- Identify departments and job roles with higher attrition.
- Analyze the impact of overtime and business travel on employee turnover.
- Examine the relationship between employee demographics and attrition.
- Explore numerical factors such as age, monthly income, total working years, and years at the company.
- Recommend strategies to improve employee retention.

## Dataset Overview
- Total Employees: 1,470
- Total Features: 35
- Employees Who Left: 237
- Overall Attrition Rate: 16.12%

## Tools & Technologies
- Python

- Pandas

- Seaborn

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Categorical Analysis
Employee attrition was analyzed across:
- Department
- Job Role
- Overtime
- Business Travel
- Gender
- Marital Status

### 2. Numerical Analysis
Analyzed attrition patterns based on:
- Age
- Monthly Income
- Total Working Years
- Years at Company
- Distance From Home

### 3. Data Visualization
Created charts and visualizations to identify employee attrition patterns and highlight workforce trends.

## Key Business Insights
The analysis explores how employee attrition varies across job roles, departments, overtime requirements, business travel frequency, and employee demographics.

It also examines how income, age, experience, and tenure relate to employee turnover.

## Business Recommendations
- Review employee retention strategies in job roles with higher attrition.
- Monitor overtime requirements and employee workload.
- Improve employee engagement and career development opportunities.
- Evaluate compensation and growth opportunities.
- Develop targeted retention initiatives based on employee attrition patterns.
